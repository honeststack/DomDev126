FAQ

Questions we get asked

We already have a warehouse. Can you fix it?

Common starting point. We audit what exists, keep what works, and migrate incrementally rather than proposing a rebuild.

Which BI tool?

Whatever your team already uses. We care that the metric layer is upstream of the tool so switching stays cheap.

How do you handle PII?

Classified at ingestion, masked in non-production, access granted by role and reviewed with your compliance owner.
