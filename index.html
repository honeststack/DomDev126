How it works

From kickoff to launch in 8–12 weeks

Fixed rhythm, weekly demos, and a working build in your hands from week three. Open a step to see what actually happens.

We pressure-test the idea against users, data and cost. You leave week one with a scoped feature set, a success metric, and a build plan you can hold us to.

Schema, service boundaries, model choice and evaluation strategy get decided before the first feature ships — the difference between an MVP you can grow and one you replace.

Two-week sprints with a demo at the end of each. Retrieval, prompts and agent flows ship behind evals, so quality is measured rather than assumed.

Load testing, security review, monitoring and runbooks. We go live with you, watch the first real traffic together, and hand over documentation your team can own.

Most clients keep the squad on after launch: new surfaces, cost tuning, model upgrades and the unglamorous reliability work that keeps a product alive.
