FAQ

Questions we get asked

What does an MVP cost?

Most land between $40k and $120k depending on integrations, compliance and whether design starts from scratch. You get a fixed number after the scoping session, not a range.

Do we own the code?

Yes — repos, infrastructure and IP are yours from the first commit, in your accounts.

What if the scope changes mid-build?

Expected. We re-cut the backlog at every sprint boundary and tell you plainly what a change costs in days.

Can you work with our engineers?

Often the best setup. We pair with your team and hand over gradually rather than building in a silo.
